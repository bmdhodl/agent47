Enforcement boundary

Recorded-budget preflight refuses the next instrumented call when stored usage is already at a cap. It does not reserve concurrent in-flight requests, predict the next response, intercept host tools, or cap a provider subscription quota.

Classes

ClassMeaning
advisoryReports or records after the fact. Does not refuse the action that just ran.
recorded-budget preflightRefuses the next instrumented action at a recorded cap. No reservation.
reservation-backedHolds capacity before in-flight work.
unsupportedNot intercepted. Do not advertise a stop here.

Supported vs remaining exposure

  • OpenAI Chat Completions and Anthropic Messages patches: recorded-budget preflight, including streamed final usage.
  • LangChain LLM callbacks: advisory. Usage is recorded after the model returns.
  • LangGraph guarded_node: recorded-budget preflight at node entry.
  • CrewAI handler: advisory. The step callback runs after the step.
  • Skillpacks and coding-agent hosts: unsupported as enforcement.
  • OpenAI Responses API: unsupported.
  • Provider subscription quota: unsupported.
