Limits

What each path stops

Recorded-budget preflight refuses the next instrumented call once stored usage is at a cap. Sync, non-streaming OpenAI Chat Completions and store-backed streams that share a StateStore reserve one call before send. In-memory and async non-stream paths do not reserve concurrent in-flight requests. Nothing here predicts the next response, intercepts host tools, or caps a provider subscription quota.

01

Classes

Every path below is one of these.

advisoryReports or records after the fact. Does not refuse the action that just ran.
recorded-budget preflightRefuses the next instrumented action at a recorded token, call, or cost cap. check() does not reserve concurrent in-flight requests.
recorded-event preflightRefuses the next instrumented action at a recorded loop, retry, timeout, or rate cap. Not a token budget.
reservation-backedHolds capacity before the request is sent.
unsupportedNot intercepted. We do not claim a stop here.
