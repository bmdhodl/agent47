Enforcement boundary

Recorded-budget preflight refuses the next instrumented call when stored usage is already at a cap. Sync, non-streaming OpenAI Chat Completions and store-backed streams that share a StateStore reserve one call before send. In-memory and async non-stream paths do not reserve concurrent in-flight requests. This does not predict the next response, intercept host tools, or cap a provider subscription quota.

Classes

ClassMeaning
advisoryReports or records after the fact. Does not refuse the action that just ran.
recorded-budget preflightRefuses the next instrumented action at a recorded token/call/cost cap. check() does not reserve concurrent in-flight requests.
recorded-event preflightRefuses the next instrumented action at a recorded loop, retry, timeout, or rate cap. Not a token budget.
reservation-backedHolds capacity before in-flight work.
unsupportedNot intercepted. Do not advertise a stop here.

Supported vs remaining exposure

  • OpenAI Chat Completions with a shared StateStore, sync and non-streaming: reserves one call before send. Not an invoice cap.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic streams with a shared StateStore: reserve one call before send. Missing usage, an early stop, or a partial usage chunk under a token or dollar cap stays unresolved.
  • In-memory patches, async non-stream calls, and Anthropic non-stream calls: recorded-budget preflight.
  • LangChain LLM callbacks: advisory. Usage is recorded after the model returns.
  • LangChain tool callbacks: recorded-budget preflight for an exhausted call budget only.
  • LangGraph guarded_node: recorded-budget preflight via consume(calls=1) at node entry. A dollar-only guard does not fire.
  • CrewAI handler: advisory. The step callback runs after the step.
  • CLI demo budget path: advisory. It records llm.result then consume().
  • Skillpacks and coding-agent hosts: unsupported as enforcement.
  • OpenAI Responses API: unsupported.
  • Provider subscription quota: unsupported.
