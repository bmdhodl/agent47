I caught a budget exception. My retry still sent another request.

AgentGuard's OpenAI and Anthropic patches were checking the budget after the response. A loop that caught BudgetExceeded could keep calling the provider after the budget was spent.

I fixed that in AgentGuard 1.3.1. The patches now check the recorded budget before sending a request, for sync and async clients.

The picture runs the same three attempts with a one-call budget:
1.3.0: three requests reached the mocked provider.
1.3.1: one request reached it. Both retries stopped before dispatch.

I also checked the real OpenAI and Anthropic clients using mocked HTTP. No paid model calls.

This check does not reserve budget for concurrent requests or predict a response's token cost. It stops a new request once recorded usage reaches the cap.
